Methodology

The Sedemson Execution Process

A rigorous 3-stage protocol guaranteeing precision tolerances, aesthetic harmony, and structural longevity.

01

Quarry Selection & Grading

We hand-select individual raw stone blocks and slabs directly at quarries, rigorously testing for density, vein continuity, and compressive strength.

02

Diamond Cutting & Dry-Laying

Using laser-guided bridge saws, slabs are fabricated to millimeter-precision. Stones are pre-assembled on a dry floor for vein and color sign-off before site delivery.

03

Master Masonry & Sealing

Experienced stonemasons install each piece using heavy-duty mechanical anchors and polymer-modified mortars, finishing with deep-penetrating fluoropolymer sealers.
