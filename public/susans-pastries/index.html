Baking Joy for Ghanaian Celebrations

Susan's Pastries & Drinks started with a simple belief: that genuine happiness is homemade. Every single pastry roll, meat pie, and cake is handcrafted with passion, using wholesome local ingredients and tested recipes to ensure the perfect crunch, moist texture, and unforgettable flavor.

  • Fresh Sourced Daily: Locally sourced produce, premium proteins & authentic spices.
